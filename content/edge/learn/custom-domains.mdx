# Using custom domains with Wasmer Edge

You can point any custom subdomains or domains to your application by **updating the DNS records** of your domain.

Please open your app in the [Wasmer dashboard](https://dashboard.wasmer.io) and click on the `Domains` tab.

You will see a list of domains that are already configured for your app.


<ImageLoader img={overview} alt="App Domains Overview" />

## Adding a new domain

To add a new domain, click on the `Add domain` button, type the domain into the `Domain` field, click on `Add domain` and follow the DNS instructions shown for the new domain.

For an apex domain such as `example.com`, or its `www` subdomain, the button reads `Continue` instead. The next step sets up the two as a pair, with `example.com` redirecting to `www.example.com`.
